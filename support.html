Starfall Run support

Free, offline, single-player space mining for iPhone.

Start a haul

Choose a local pilot name, play the guided first haul, mine an asteroid, return to base and spend earned credits on upgrades. Sol and Centauri unlock through delivered-ore progression.

Sound

Tap a game control to start audio. Check the in-game volume, iPhone volume and silent switch. Calls and backgrounding pause gameplay; resume deliberately when you return.

Progress

Progress stays on this device. Return to base and close normally when possible. Interrupted flights can be recovered from a checkpoint. Keep the app installed; there is no developer account or cross-device recovery service.

Performance

Use Smooth graphics and Calm camera in Settings if needed. If graphics are interrupted, reopen the app.

Contact us

Contact DeltaVStudios@outlook.com for support.

For an issue, include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, mining region and short reproduction steps. Avoid sharing private data.
