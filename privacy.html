Starfall Run privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026. This policy covers the iPhone edition, including its optional online advertising integration. Gameplay and local saves work offline.

Your game stays on your device

Your local pilot nickname, progress, earned credits, in-game purchases, settings and interrupted-flight checkpoint are stored on your iPhone. No account is required. In-game purchases use credits earned through play, not real money.

What we collect

The developer does not receive your pilot nickname, saved progress, cargo history, precise location or health information. There is no developer gameplay analytics service, account login, real-money purchasing service, social feature or remote game-code updating in this edition. The optional native advertising integration uses Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Advertising and privacy choices

When advertising is enabled and an internet connection is available, Google’s advertising and consent SDKs may process IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers where available, advertising interactions, crash/performance diagnostics and consent choices. We do not pass your nickname, save, cargo history or other private gameplay content to the advertising SDK. Non-personalized ads are requested and Publisher first-party ID is disabled; this does not eliminate all advertising data processing. This version does not request Apple tracking permission.

Applicable consent messages are handled through UMP before ads are requested. When required, Settings includes Ad privacy choices so you can review those choices. If consent information cannot be refreshed, ads do not load and the game remains playable. Google processes advertising data under its own privacy policy; see also how Google uses information from partner apps. Test builds may display Google sample ads. Public builds may request ads from the configured AdMob units. Advertising requests can process data in either mode.

Saves and deletion

Keep the app installed to preserve progress. Removing the app and its data can erase local saves. There is no developer cloud account, cross-device sync or guaranteed recovery after deletion. Apple device backups may include app data according to your device settings; this is not a developer-operated cloud save service.

Support and Apple services

If you choose to contact support, the information you provide is used to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive personal information. Opening the public support site or a mail app uses that service’s normal networking and privacy practices. Apple may process App Store, diagnostics and backup information under your Apple settings and Apple’s own privacy policy.

Children

The game has no chat, player trading or user-generated content sharing. Use a nickname rather than personal information for local pilots. The advertising integration requests general-audience ad content; this is not a claim of Kids Category certification. Third-party advertising data processing is described above.
